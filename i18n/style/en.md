# English style
- Warm, short, plain. Second person ("you"). Avoid hype and exclamation marks.
- Keep sentences short; prefer active voice. American spelling.
- Faith language: gentle and respectful; Bible quotations use NIV wording only when the source quotes scripture.
- Prices: "$14.99". Never convert KRW amounts in the source; keep numbers exactly as given unless the key is a price key.
